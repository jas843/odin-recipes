Pancit Canton

Back to Home

A Filipino stir-fried noodle dish, often served at birthdays for long life.

Ingredients

  • 250 g pancit canton noodles
  • 200 g chicken, sliced
  • 1 carrot, sliced thin
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce

Steps

  1. Sauté garlic and chicken until cooked.
  2. Add carrot and cabbage, then stir-fry for 3 minutes.
  3. Pour in 2 cups of water and soy sauce, then bring to a boil.
  4. Add noodles and toss until the liquid is absorbed.
