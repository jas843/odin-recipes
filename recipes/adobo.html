Chicken Adobo

Back to Home

A classic Filipino dish of chicken simmered in soy sauce and vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar

Steps

  1. Combine all ingredients in a pot.
  2. Simmer until the chicken is tender.
